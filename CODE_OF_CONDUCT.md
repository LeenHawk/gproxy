## English

### Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in
the GPROXY community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

### Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes and learning from them
- Being patient with newcomers and people writing in a second language

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  account identifiers, API keys, tokens or cookies, without their explicit
  permission
- Asking for or sharing ways to abuse, resell or evade the terms of upstream
  providers' accounts belonging to others
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

### Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — the repository,
issues, pull requests, security advisories, documentation site and any other
official channel — and when an individual is officially representing the
project in public spaces.

### Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the maintainer at <leenhawk@leenhawk.com>. All complaints will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of
the reporter will be respected.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and may
apply the following consequences:

1. **Correction** — a private, written warning explaining the violation.
2. **Warning** — a warning with consequences for continued behavior, including
   a period of no interaction with the people involved.
3. **Temporary ban** — a temporary ban from any interaction or public
   communication with the community.
4. **Permanent ban** — a permanent ban from any public interaction within the
   community.

### Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1,
available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html>.

## 简体中文

### 我们的承诺

身为社区成员、贡献者和维护者，我们承诺：无论年龄、体型、可见或不可见的残障、
民族、性征、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育程度、社会经济地位、国籍、外貌、
种族、宗教信仰、性取向或性身份如何，参与 GPROXY 社区的每个人都不会受到骚扰。

我们承诺以有助于建设开放、友善、多元、包容和健康社区的方式行事和互动。

### 我们的准则

有助于营造良好环境的行为包括：

- 尊重不同的意见、观点和经历
- 提出建设性意见，并以良好心态接受他人的建设性意见
- 以社区和项目的整体利益为重
- 为自己的错误负责，并从中学习
- 对新人以及使用非母语交流的人保持耐心

不可接受的行为包括：

- 使用带有性暗示的语言或图像，以及任何形式的性关注或性骚扰
- 挑衅、侮辱或贬损性评论，以及人身攻击或政治攻击
- 公开或私下的骚扰
- 未经明确许可，公开他人的私人信息，例如住址、邮箱、账号标识、API Key、
  Token 或 Cookie
- 索取或分享滥用、转售他人上游服务商账号，或规避其服务条款的方法
- 其他在专业场合中可被合理认为不恰当的行为

### 适用范围

本准则适用于所有项目空间——代码仓库、Issue、Pull Request、安全公告、文档站点
及其他官方渠道——也适用于个人在公共场合代表本项目的情形。

### 执行

如遇辱骂、骚扰或其他不可接受的行为，可发邮件至 <leenhawk@leenhawk.com>
向维护者举报。所有举报都会得到及时、公正的审查和处理，举报者的隐私和安全将受到尊重。

维护者有权删除、编辑或拒绝不符合本准则的评论、提交、代码、Issue 及其他贡献，
并可采取以下措施：

1. **纠正**——私下书面提醒，说明违规之处。
2. **警告**——发出警告并说明继续违规的后果，包括一段时间内不得与相关人员互动。
3. **临时封禁**——在一段时间内禁止与社区进行任何互动或公开交流。
4. **永久封禁**——永久禁止在社区内进行任何公开互动。
