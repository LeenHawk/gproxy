## v4.2.6

> AI Studio Playground now mints its anti-bot proof itself, with no external signing service. This release also adds Gemini XHIGH and media inspection options and aligns the Antigravity channel with CLI 1.5.12.
>
> AI Studio Playground 现在自行生成反机器人校验，不再需要外部签名服务。本次还新增 Gemini XHIGH 与媒体检查选项，并将 Antigravity 渠道对齐到 CLI 1.5.12。

### English

#### Channels

- AI Studio Playground mints its WAA/BotGuard proof in process. A generation previously required a `proof_server_url` pointing at a separate signing service that received the credential's cookies and the request contents. The channel now fetches the challenge and runs Google's interpreter in the same Firefox DOM environment itself, on an embedded QuickJS engine, so `proof_server_url` is gone and no external service is needed. The signer is native only; the channel is unavailable on the edge (Worker) build.
- Antigravity is aligned with CLI 1.5.12, and its Neon model prices are refreshed.

#### Credential login

- Completing an authorization-code login accepts the `code#state` text that a callback page shows for copying. Claude's callback page now shows only this text instead of leaving `code` and `state` in the address bar, and the console refused it as a callback without state. Paste it into the callback field as is; a full callback URL still works.

#### Protocol

- Gemini requests accept the XHIGH thinking level, and media parts carry inspection options.

### 简体中文

#### 渠道

- AI Studio Playground 在进程内生成 WAA/BotGuard 校验。此前一次生成需要配置 `proof_server_url` 指向一个单独的签名服务，并把凭证 Cookie 与请求内容发给它。现在渠道自己拉取 challenge，并在内嵌的 QuickJS 引擎上、于同样的 Firefox DOM 环境里运行 Google 的解释器，因此 `proof_server_url` 已移除，也不再需要外部服务。该签名器仅在原生构建可用，渠道在边缘（Worker）构建上不可用。
- Antigravity 渠道对齐到 CLI 1.5.12，并刷新其 Neon 模型价格。

#### 凭证登录

- 完成授权码登录时接受回调页面显示的 `code#state` 文本。Claude 的回调页面现在只显示这段文本，不再把 `code` 和 `state` 留在地址栏，控制台此前会因缺少 state 而拒绝。把它原样粘贴到回调输入框即可，完整回调 URL 仍然可用。

#### 协议

- Gemini 请求支持 XHIGH 思考级别，媒体部分可携带检查选项。
